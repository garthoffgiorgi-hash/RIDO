## ⚠️ Contradictions to resolve before this ships

The page states **four different "driver keeps" instances** (three distinct figures), and none is
labelled with what it nets out. Verified against a fresh extraction of the export, not memory:

| Where | Claim | What it appears to net out |
|---|---|---|
| Hero stat tile | "Drivers keep **87%** of the average fare" | Commission + the $50 flat fee, at $5,000/mo volume ($4,400 − $50 ≈ 87%) |
| Hero ride-card example | "$7.30 kept (**87%**)" on an $8.40 fare | Reuses the stat tile's 87% as a proof-point fare — not independently derivable. A single $8.40 ride can't carry a monthly $50 fee, and no tier maps to 87% per-ride (tiers imply 80% / 88% / 92%) |
| Economics section | "$4,200 kept · driver keeps **84%**" on $5,000 of fares | Commission + flat fee + card processing (~2.9%) |
| The model itself | **88%** | Commission only: $1,000×20% + $2,000×12% + $2,000×8% = $600 on $5,000 |

Four problems, in order of severity:

1. **The "$1,240 more" headline doesn't reconcile with the page's own numbers next to it.**
   $4,200 (the displayed 84% figure) minus $3,160 (the incumbent figure) is **$1,040, not
   $1,240.** The $1,240 actually used only matches the *unlabeled* 88%/$4,400 commission-only
   figure: $4,400 − $3,160 = $1,240 exactly. So the headline is quietly built on a bigger, better
   number than the one displayed two lines above it — the inconsistency compounds rather than
   explains itself.
2. **"87% of the average fare" is not a fare figure.** A monthly subscription fee can't be netted
   out of a single fare. Per-fare, a driver keeps 80% (tier 1) to 92% (tier 3) — 87% isn't any of
   them.
3. **During the pilot the flat fee is $0** (ADR-0003), so the launch-accurate number is 88% — the
   page understates RIDO's own advantage while launching.
4. **84% and 87% contradict each other** on the same page with no stated basis for either.

Independent of what the page itself computed: recomputed cleanly from the actual model at
$5,000/mo GMV — driver keeps $4,400 (88%, commission only) — the advantage over an incumbent is
**$1,150 against a 35% incumbent and $1,400 against 40%.** ($3,160/63% on the page implies a
~36.8% incumbent take, which is a real, separately-correct number — it just isn't what produces
the page's own $1,240.)

**Root cause, and the fix — done.** `docs/business/monetization.md` now has a "Published
driver-keeps figure" section: ~86%, commission-only, pilot-accurate. **This page needs rebuilding
against that figure, not the 87/84/88% it currently shows** — and its $5,000/mo example should be
reconciled with monetization.md's $3,600/mo example (that doc flags this explicitly) before
either number ships. This was exactly the drift ADR-0004 exists to stop.
